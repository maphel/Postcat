No Postcat account required. Open https://example.com/ and select Postcat in DevTools (check the overflow menu). Click Start recording, then Allow capture. In Postcat Options, turn off XHR / Fetch only. Hard reload (Ctrl+Shift+R or Command+Shift+R). Select the GET for https://example.com/ and click Send. To test storage, click Save to collection and choose and confirm a passphrase of at least 16 characters. Lock and unlock the collection in Saved.
